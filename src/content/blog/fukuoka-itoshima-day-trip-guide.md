When traveling in Fukuoka, be sure to reserve one day for Itoshima. A quiet coastal town loved by locals, Itoshima boasts soothing shorelines and the famous floating ocean torii gate. It is one of the best laid-back day trip destinations in Fukuoka, perfect for a relaxing seaside escape.

Most online Itoshima travel guides are generic and repetitive, only recommending overhyped tourist spots that are always crowded and often disappointing. Based on my on-site experience, I've put together a practical, hassle-free one-day itinerary to help you explore Itoshima effortlessly.

This guide solves two biggest travel pain points for visitors: detailed public transport routes from central Fukuoka to Itoshima, and crowd-free hidden spots to enjoy exclusive ocean views and budget-friendly fresh seafood. Full of actionable tips, this itinerary is easy for first-time travelers to follow.

## Transport Guide: How to Get to Itoshima from Fukuoka

Itoshima is easily accessible by public transport, but confusing entrances can waste your travel time. Start your journey at **Higashi-Hakata Station**. Head to the Hakata Exit and follow the clear signage leading to **Hakata Bus Terminal**.

Enter the terminal through the building's distinctive teal main entrance. Skip the staircases and take the elevator directly to the **3rd-floor waiting area**.

![Hakata Bus Terminal entrance with teal facade, bus bound for Futamigaura](/images/5141.jpg)

Look for **Gate 32**, the dedicated platform for buses bound for Futamigaura. This direct bus takes you straight to the Itoshima ocean torii area with no transfers, ideal for independent travelers.

> **Important Travel Reminder:** This bus route stops operating after 2 PM. Always check the latest daily timetable in advance to avoid missing your ride and ruining your itinerary.

The bus ride takes approximately one hour. Alight at the **Futamigaura Meotoiwa Mae** stop. Cross the road, and you will arrive at the core scenic area with stunning views of the floating torii gate and Meotoiwa Couple Rocks.

***

## Torii Gate Photography: How to Avoid Crowds for Perfect Shots

The Itoshima floating torii gate is the landmark attraction of the area. It gets crowded during noon time and holidays — but you typically only need to wait a few minutes to capture clean, unobstructed photos.

![The iconic floating torii gate at Futamigaura with Meotoiwa couple rocks in the ocean](/images/555.jpg)

Right next to the scenic area stands the viral Itoshima cafeteria. It constantly has extremely long queues, and customer reviews are mixed with mediocre cost performance. **It is not worth your precious travel time.**

For a better travel experience, skip the crowded tourist hotspots and try these local hidden spots to enjoy the authentic coastal vibe of Itoshima.

***

## Hidden Scenic Spot: Private Ocean View Terrace with Zero Crowds

Next to the busy Itoshima cafeteria lies a tiny local shop called **Zekkei**. This underrated hidden gem is missed by most tourists.

Follow the staircase beside the shop downstairs, and you will find a peaceful secret terrace far away from the noisy main scenic area.

This self-service unmanned shop features front-row window seats overlooking the entire ocean, offering an unblocked panoramic sea view. You can pick your favorite snacks and drinks, pay via self-checkout, and sit back and relax. Hardly any tourists visit here. You can enjoy the sea breeze and peaceful ocean views without crowds, offering a far more comfortable experience than the overhyped spots upstairs.

## Food Guide: Budget Ocean-View Seafood Bowl (No Waiting)

After visiting the torii gate and hidden viewpoint, it's time for the highlight of this day trip — delicious and affordable seafood with stunning ocean views. Skip the crowded tourist restaurants and enjoy a premium dining experience like a local.

Take a bus from the roadside stop near the scenic area, heading toward **Kyudai Gakuen Toshi Station** or **Hakata Tenjin**. After a 10-minute ride, get off at the **Zauo Honten Mae** bus stop to reach this hidden food spot.

Walk backward for around 50 meters, and you will easily spot **Zauo Honten** with clear roadside signs.

![Premium salmon seafood rice bowl with tempura set meal at Zauo Honten, ocean-view dining](/images/44.png)

> **Dining Tip:** Make sure to sit on the second floor! The entire upper floor features unbeatable front-facing sea views, letting you dine while admiring the endless ocean scenery.

While nearby tourist restaurants charge over 2,300 JPY for a single seafood bowl, Zauo Honten offers premium salmon rice bowls and tempura set meals for only around **1,000 JPY**. Every dish is flavorful and generously portioned with amazing value for money.

Best of all, there is almost no waiting time. I visited and practically had the entire sea-view dining area to myself. It delivers a high-end ocean-view dining experience at a local budget price, making it a must-visit restaurant in Itoshima.

***

## Coastal Walk: Relaxing Odahama Beach

The restaurant is adjacent to quiet **Odahama Beach**, a local coastline free of commercial crowds. It is a favorite leisure spot for Fukuoka locals to unwind and enjoy peaceful seaside moments.

![Odahama Beach with a uniquely shaped tree stretching toward the ocean, peaceful coastal scenery](/images/362.png)

A uniquely shaped tree stretching toward the ocean stands on the shore, creating incredibly dreamy scenery perfect for casual photos. Mostly visited by locals, this quiet beach is ideal for a slow stroll after meals.

***

## Return Guide: Travel Back to Central Fukuoka

After a full day of sightseeing, dining, and coastal walking, head back from the same bus stop where you alighted.

You can take a direct bus back to Hakata Bus Terminal, or ride to Kyudai Gakuen Toshi Station and transfer to a JR train returning to central Fukuoka. Choose the route based on your accommodation location for maximum convenience.

***

## Final Thoughts

Itoshima is absolutely an underrated day trip treasure in Fukuoka. You can enjoy a slow-paced journey, avoid crowded commercial tourist traps, discover hidden scenic spots, and taste cost-effective ocean-view seafood. With its soothing coastal scenery and relaxed vibe, it is the perfect destination for travelers who prefer quiet, crowd-free travel experiences.
